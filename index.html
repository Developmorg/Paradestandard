How it was built

Built together,
to one standard.

Parade Standard is the two of us — Morgan and Charlotte. We spent two years working across Cornwall, fell for this stretch of coast, and decided to build something of our own here.

Morgan brings the discipline: former British Army, and an ex-Physical Training Instructor — years where “good enough” never was. Charlotte brings the business sense and the eye for finish, having run her own hairdressing business — she knows better than anyone that the details, the presentation, and how you make people feel are everything.

Between us, that’s the whole promise: military standards and a genuine feel for how a property should look and welcome its guests. We treat every property as if the next arrival were our own — because to us, that’s exactly how it should be.

FAQs

Good to know.

Do you cover my area?

We’re based in St Ives and cover St Ives and Carbis Bay as our core area. We’re happy to travel a little further for the right property — if you’re nearby, just ask.

What’s included in a changeover?

A complete reset between guests: every room cleaned to inspection standard, beds remade, bathrooms deep-cleaned, surfaces checked and restocked, and the whole property photographed on completion so you have proof it’s guest-ready.

Do you supply linen?

We supply linen through Star Linen in St Ives, so it’s always fresh and professionally laundered. If you’d prefer to keep your own, we offer a pick-up and drop-off service too — as long as you’re not too far away.

How do I get a quote?

Tell us about your property using the form below, or give us a call. Every property is different, so we quote per property and get back to you quickly.

Our Services

Everything your let
needs, in one place.

Parade Standard looks after the whole property, not just the clean. Pick the services you need — from our flagship changeover to full hands-off care — and we handle the rest, to inspection standard, every time.

Flagship

Changeover Cleaning

Our core service and the heart of what we do. Between every guest we completely reset the property — every room cleaned to inspection standard, beds remade, bathrooms deep-cleaned, surfaces checked, and the whole property photographed on completion so you have proof it is guest-ready. Your Saturday turnaround, guaranteed, every time.

Linen — Pick-Up & Drop-Off

We supply all linen through Star Linen in St Ives — fresh, professionally laundered and ready for every stay. If you’d rather use your own, we also offer a pick-up and drop-off laundry service, provided you’re within reasonable distance.

Restocking & Welcome

We keep your essentials topped up — tea, coffee, toiletries, cleaning supplies — and set up a warm welcome, so guests walk into a property that feels cared for from the moment they arrive.

Key-Holding & Property Checks

We securely hold a set of keys and check on your property between lets — spotting anything amiss, airing rooms, and giving you peace of mind that your investment is looked after even when it is empty.

Deep & Spring Cleans

Beyond the regular changeover, we offer thorough seasonal deep cleans — inside cupboards and appliances, behind furniture, limescale, the lot — to keep your property immaculate and protect its long-term condition.

Maintenance & Repairs

If something is broken or worn, we flag it to you first with a photo. Then — only if you would like — we arrange a trusted local tradesperson to put it right, and oversee the job so you do not have to.

How pricing works: every property is different, so we quote per property based on size, turnaround needs and the services you choose — most owners find us competitive, with a noticeably higher standard.
